My Dearest Husband,

Sometimes I sit quietly & wonder how Allah wrote someone like you into my destiny so beautifully. It still feels unreal that I can finally call you mine.

The day of our Nikkah feels both like a distant dream and the realest thing that has ever happened to me. In one sacred moment, everything changed. I became yours, and you became mine — in the sight of Allah, witnessed by angels, sealed with a promise my heart already knew before my lips could say qubool hai.

I used to wonder what it felt like to be truly seen by someone. Not just looked at — but seen. The way you look at me, as though I carry something worth protecting, as though my thoughts matter — that is something I never imagined I deserved until you showed me I did.

You have never made me feel small. Not once. In a world that so often asks a woman to shrink herself, you have always made room for me — for my dreams, my opinions, my ambitions, my restless nights when ideas keep me awake. You listen. Fully. Without impatience.

I feel safe with you. Not safe in a small, quiet way — but safe in the way that lets me be entirely myself. Safe enough to cry without explanation. Safe enough to dream out loud and know you will walk beside me toward every one of those dreams.

You respect me. Not just in words spoken in gentle moments, but in the quiet ways nobody else would notice — the way you ask my thoughts, the way you acknowledge my feelings even when they inconvenience you. You honour me. And I feel it every single day.

I think about us growing old together, and it fills me with a warmth I cannot describe. I imagine us in a home made of duas and laughter and tea shared in the mornings. I imagine the way your hand will feel in mine decades from now — familiar, steady, warm.

Every evening I raise my hands in dua, and your name is always first. I ask Allah to protect you, to give you ease, to fill your life with barakah so abundant it overflows into everything you touch. You deserve the most beautiful version of this life.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for being patient with my imperfections. Thank you for reminding me of my worth on the days I forget it. Thank you for being exactly who you are.

You are my home. My calm. My answered prayer. And I would choose you — in every lifetime — without hesitation.

Forever yours, Your Wife 💕 🌹 ✦ 💍 ✦ 🌙
